Who We Are

Whitemars is an Egypt-based electric mobility brand focused on compact electric vehicles designed for controlled environments such as compounds, resorts, campuses, and large facilities. We deliver ready-to-operate vehicles, supported by onboarding, maintenance support, and spare parts availability, helping organizations and individuals adopt electric mobility with confidence.

  • Purpose-built for controlled environments (compounds, resorts, campuses)
  • Deployment-ready delivery with onboarding and operational support
  • Local maintenance support and spare parts availability
